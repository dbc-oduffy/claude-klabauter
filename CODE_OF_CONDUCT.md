# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant
v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Please read the full text at the link above. By participating in this project, you agree to
abide by its terms.

In short: be kind, be constructive, be specific. Assume the person on the other side of the
thread is trying to do good work with incomplete information — because so are you.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, open an issue or contact the maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
