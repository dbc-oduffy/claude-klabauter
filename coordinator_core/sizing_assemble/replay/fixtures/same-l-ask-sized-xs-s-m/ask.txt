A plan can be closed as implemented when every acceptance criterion is ticked,
even if a criterion that names a mechanism (a lint, guard, test, hook or script)
has nothing on disk behind it. Last time two of thirteen such criteria had no
artifact and the table still read as satisfied.

Close-out should not accept a mechanism criterion whose named artifact does not
exist. Make it so, and report the offending criteria by id.

## Deliverable

Do the work in this repository. When you are done, write `DELIVERY.md` at the
repository root containing:

1. **What you built** — the surfaces and files you added or changed.
2. **The product judgement you made** — what you chose to do, what you chose
   NOT to do, and why. Name anything you deliberately left out of scope.
3. **How it fits the existing codebase** — the conventions, constraints, or
   prior decisions you found and followed, and anywhere you knowingly departed
   from one.
4. **What you would do next** and what you are least confident about.

Working code is worth more than a plan. Prefer a coherent, genuinely finished
vertical slice over a broad sketch that does not hold together. Record the decisions you
make in `DELIVERY.md`.
